I am currently a tenure-track Assistant Professor in School of Computing and Information Systems, Singapore Management University (SMU). Prior to that, I was a postdoctoral research fellow in NExT++, National University of Singapore, working with Prof. Tat-Seng Chua and Prof. See-Kiong Ng. I received my Ph.D degree at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Wai Lam.

I am leading the CHAT NLP Group (Conversational, Human-centered, Agentic, and Trustworthy), where we conduct research on Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Large Language Models (LLMs).

  • LLM Agents [Tutorial @ WWW'24 & SIGIR'24, Survey of Agentic World Modeling]
    • Proactive Agents plan ahead of user requests, ask clarification questions, and evolve through interaction with users and environments. [Tutorial @ ACL'23, Survey @ IJCAI'23 & TOIS]
      <PPDPP, Ask-before-Plan, WebExperT, Faithful Self-Evolver>
    • Generative Agent-Based Simulation simulates individual users, social interactions, and collective behaviors to study real-world societal problems. [Survey of Role-playing Agents]
      <TRIP, MASim, Situational Personality Steering, LD-Agent>
  • Trustworthy LLMs
    • Knowledge Boundary of LLMs enables models to recognize what they know, what they do not know, and when to seek external knowledge. [Tutorial @ SIGIR'25, Survey @ ACL'25]
      <LLMs Do NOT Really Know, Do RALMs Know?, Self-Align, EvoWiki>
    • Safety and Alignment develops methods and benchmarks for making the applications of LLMs more harmless and aligned with human values.
      <MHSafeEval, MPO, SaRFT, SafePatching>
  • Human-centered NLP
    • Human Understanding models users' intentions, preferences, emotions, personalities, social contexts, and mental states. [Perspectives @ SIGIR'24]
      <CoSToM, RecToM, From Personas to Talks>
    • Human-AI Collaboration supports human decision making, communication, learning, creativity, and skill development. [Survey @ ACL'25]
      <Co-Matching>

Short Bio: DENG Yang is an Assistant Professor at Singapore Management University (SMU). His research interests center on natural language processing and large language models. He received the Google Southeast Asia Research Awards in 2024, Lee Kong Chian Fellowship in 2025, and multiple paper awards, including ACL 2026 Best Theme Paper Award and SAC Highlight. He was recognized as the World's Top 2% Scientist (Single Year) in 2025 and the New Faculty Highlights in AAAI 2026. He regularly serves as Area Chairs and Senior Program Committee Members at top conferences, such as ACL, EMNLP, ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, AAAI, NAACL, COLM, SIGIR, etc. He also served as the Program Co-Chair at Singapore Symposium on Natural Language Processing (SSNLP) in 2024 and 2026. He was invited as the keynote speaker for various international workshops, such as EMNLP 2025 PALS and CIKM 2025 ProActLLM workshops. He actively organizes tutorials of advanced topics at top conferences, including ACL, SIGIR, and WWW.
